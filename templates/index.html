About Project

Biotech AI Teacher is an educational web application developed to help biotechnology students understand complex biological concepts in a simple and interactive way.

The website provides explanations, applications, and viva questions for important biotechnology topics such as DNA, RNA, PCR, CRISPR, ELISA, Bioinformatics, and more.
